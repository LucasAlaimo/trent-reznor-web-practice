About

Trent Reznor is an American musician, composer, and producer best known as the founder and creative force behind Nine Inch Nails, one of the most influential industrial rock bands. Throughout his career, he has been recognized for his innovative sound and artistic vision. In addition to his work in music, Reznor has composed acclaimed film scores alongside Atticus Ross, earning recognition for their contributions to modern cinema.
